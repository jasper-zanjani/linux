The most basic document class is **article**, but many are available on [CTAN](https://www.ctan.org/topic/class):

The **article** class defines [five levels of section](https://nasa.github.io/nasa-latex-docs/html/examples/section.html):

- `\section`
- `\subsection`
- `\subsubsection`
- `\paragraph`
- `\subparagraph`
