The **Management Information Base (MIB)** is a universal, hierarchical, dictionary-like database used to manage network device data.

Objects in the MIB are organized into **modules** or sets of related managed objects defined by a technical language called [Structure of Management Information (SMI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_of_Management_Information).

Each entry in the MIB is addressed using a series of integers separated by periods called an [**object-identifier (OID)**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object_identifier).

- The MIB tree has an unlabeled root with three subtrees, one of which is the Enterprise subtree 1. The Internet is 1.3.6.1
